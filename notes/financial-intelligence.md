# Financial intelligence

Implementation policy / source map for the October 2026 financial-dashboard specification, based on main `3f1cc0e`. Existing finance administration and all historical ledgers remain authoritative; `/app/finance/dashboard` is an independent read/report destination. No production deployment is authorized.

## Sources and semantics

| Measure | Source and formula | Date / exclusions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current obligations / collected / outstanding | Existing collections: amount, net paid, amount minus paid | Current snapshot; cancelled obligations separated. Archived clients retained. |
| Collection completion | Net paid / active obligation amount, same eligible cohort | Never divide unrelated period payments by period-issued charges. |
| Issued in period | Actual collections, commercial billing date or general creation date | Selected inclusive UTC calendar dates; no pricing proposals or future agreement multiplication. |
| Gross / reversed / net collections | Positive payments minus positive linked reversals | `paid_on` / `reversed_on`, independently of entry timestamps; includes retained cancelled payments, shown separately from active balances. |
| Aging / deadlines | Current active outstanding and nullable due dates | UTC today; exclusive buckets, missing dates separate. Future windows are cumulative and explicitly labelled. |
| Client / service contribution | Same authorized collection cohort and selected activity | Services only when agreements.view is current; otherwise unclassified. No website allocation. |
| Partner position | Immutable period terms and existing allocation::position | Current net customer payments, actual collector and signed retained internal transfers; positive due to ROISE, negative payable. |
| Operating expenses | New independent retained expense obligations and positive payments/reversals | Expense date, payment date and due date remain distinct. Customer payments and partner transfers are never copied here. |
| Recorded organizational cash movement | Known ROISE customer receipts less matching reversals, incoming/outgoing settlements, paid operating expenses less expense reversals | Activity dates. Unknown historical collectors excluded and disclosed. No opening/bank balance or accounting profit is claimed. |
| Outlook | Existing outstanding due dates, optionally scaled by an explicit percentage | Deterministic collection scenario, not newly issued debt or guaranteed cash. Future agreement estimates remain separate from obligations. |

Global `finance.read_company` permits entering company reports but does not replace `clients.view` + `billing.view` on every contributing client. Commercial terms/partners additionally need agreements.view. MCP requires finance:read plus current permissions and its optional brand ceiling. Operating expense access requires new global expenses.view; managing requires expenses.manage plus view. Linked expenses additionally need current client and billing view. Internal company expenses are excluded from brand-scoped MCP. Existing roles are unchanged except the designated full administrator receives new catalog keys.

## Retained expense rules

Expense title, category, vendor, description, client, currency, original amount and dates are immutable. Correct an unused obligation by explicit cancellation and a new record. Payment/reversal events are positive, append-only, currency-bound, revision-checked, capped against remaining balance/original payment, and deduplicated by actor + exact normalized command. A reversal restores payable exposure; it never erases a payment. Cancellation requires no payment history. No automatic recurring expenses, tax invoices or fiscal accounting claims. Category creation is explicit and extensible; arbitrary stored user text never enters audits. Supporting request evidence cannot safely be reparented to expenses and is not reused as a public document store.

## Reporting bounds and temporal clarity

Each report reads one SQLite transaction. Position and activity are separately labelled; past date filters do not claim reconstructed historical balances. Exact checked i128 SQLite sums return canonical strings. Activity windows and chart buckets are bounded; complete reports refuse excessive working sets instead of returning partial totals. Details use bounded keyset pages. Exports contain one authorized page with continuation metadata and fixed definitions; arbitrary names are CSV-formula escaped. Fresh reads bypass Redis until a reliable complete-ledger revision can bind cache keys; no stale TTL-only financial cache. Private reports never enter the public asset service worker.

No FX, recognized accounting revenue/profit, complete bank balance, inferred legacy collector, credit score or historical receivable curve is available. Explicit collection scenarios and recorded cash surplus are labelled accordingly. Actual paid costs have incomplete organization coverage unless the operator has recorded all spending; no coverage percentage can prove missing costs.

## API, navigation and operation

`GET /api/v1/finance/dashboard` returns one internally consistent reporting snapshot. Core aggregates, source-bound checks, partner source periods, and customer/settlement cash series share one SQLite statement and its materialized eligible obligation/event/settlement sets. Each reporting statement materializes current brand authority once before joining that brand’s obligations, avoiding repeated identical grant checks per fee. Only prepared query plans are cached; bound actors, grants and financial rows are read afresh in each transaction. The transaction also computes authorized commercial allocations and expenses using the existing exact allocation policy. `GET /api/v1/finance/reports/{receivables|payments|clients}` supplies stable UUID-keyset pages. The matching `exports` routes return bounded CSV pages; additional service, partner, aging, monthly cash and expense exports reuse those same currently authorized sources.

Filters: currency, inclusive from/to, client_id, service_type, agreement_id, partner_id, current obligation status, method, actual collector, due_from/due_to, human-readable q and day/week/month/quarter/year trend interval. Position uses current balances; event activity uses the selected business dates. Current record attributes also filter historical activity, so this is not an immutable historical state reconstruction. Comparisons use an equal-length prior window plus prior-year calendar dates; February 29 clamps to February 28. All time starts at the supported calendar minimum and has no prior comparison. Cumulative net collections reconcile with period activity. Concentration uses positive client net-collection contributions as its explicit denominator; negative client cohorts are excluded from that denominator.

Limits: 50,000 eligible source obligations per currency/client selection; 50,000 payment, reversal and internal-settlement events per inspected activity window; 5,000 commercial periods; 10,000 agreement or contributing-client groups; 366 chart buckets; 100 expense category/vendor groups. Oversized complete results fail with a narrowing instruction. Detail pages default to 25, at most 100; CSV is at most 512 KiB. Partner and expense exports expose continuation cursors. Separate pages are fresh snapshots and do not promise a fixed multi-page historical export. Financial cache correctness is achieved by fresh SQLite reads; no Redis entry can survive a financial mutation because this reporting layer deliberately does not cache mutable ledgers. Existing immutable provider caching remains unchanged.

Expenses use `/api/v1/finance/expenses`, `expenses/{id}`, `expenses/{id}/events`, `expenses/{id}/cancel` and `expense-categories`. Create a category, record the actual expense, then record partial/full payments. A reversal references its original payment and requires an explanation. An unused expense can be explicitly cancelled. In the dashboard, selecting a client links new expenses to that client; all-clients selection records internal organization costs. Current record revisions remain pinned in an editing form; review history and explicitly discard/reload a stale draft. The workspace-level confirmation retains an unresolved command across route navigation. Never create a replacement command merely because a network response was lost.

Cash movement is unavailable when service, partner, agreement, status, due-date, text, method or collector filters would create incompatible customer/expense cohorts. An authorized client filter can show that client's recorded movements, with organization-internal expenses excluded. Missing expense permission yields restricted coverage, never a fabricated zero. Known costs alone cannot establish complete organizational profitability. No opening balance, accounting revenue recognition, market FX, fiscal invoicing, recurring expense commitments, private expense document store or inferred website finance is introduced.

Migration `000009_financial_intelligence.sql` is additive and checksummed; four STRICT expense tables, retained-history triggers and currency/date indexes extend schema 8 to 9. Existing user/client/website/task/financial identifiers and prior migrations are unchanged. Recovery inventories and exact ledger verification include these tables. Index creation scans existing tables during the upgrade window; back up before upgrading. No automatic downgrade, history deletion, production migration or production deployment is authorized.

Attention rules are deterministic and bounded: positive outstanding over 90 days or without a due date; a largest client contributing at least 50% of positive client net collections; reversals at least 10% of period gross collections (or positive reversals with zero gross); incomplete collector attribution; unsettled partner receivables/payables; and recorded expense payments due in 30 days. They are review conditions, not credit scores, accounting judgments or automatic notifications. At most 25 conditions are shown. Detailed tables sort their current bounded page using exact values; UUID-keyset traversal and CSV source ordering remain unchanged.
